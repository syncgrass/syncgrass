Executive summary

A trusted spatial record for government, citizens and markets.

GeoLandTrace digitises the land administration lifecycle—from survey and parcel creation through application, verification, valuation, consent, allocation, registration and certificate issuance. It gives land agencies a common data model for the legal, spatial, documentary and financial dimensions of property.

The platform is designed for ministries, land bureaus, cadastral offices, survey departments, planning authorities and development programmes. Public-facing services reduce uncertainty for applicants, while executive intelligence reveals workloads, revenue, land-use activity and service performance.

Strategic valueA dependable land information foundation for planning, investment and governance.
Operational valueDigitised workflows, transparent queues and fewer disconnected records.
Public valueClearer application status, more consistent services and defensible records.

Security & integrity

Protect the public record at every layer

Access, changes, approvals, documents and issued instruments are governed through an explicit control model aligned to institutional responsibilities.

  • Role, department, geography and transaction-level access controls
  • SSO through OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Complete audit history for parcel, party, document and workflow changes
  • Electronic certificate verification and configurable digital signing
  • Backups, disaster recovery, retention and archival policies
