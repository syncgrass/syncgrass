Executive summary

Run the property from one commercial and operational truth.

HotelSync brings the guest lifecycle and hotel operating cycle into one platform. Reservations flow into room allocation, check-in, guest folios, housekeeping, maintenance, POS charges, payments and relationship management without repeated data entry.

General managers gain a live view of occupancy, revenue, service delivery and cost signals. Front-office and operational teams work from shared status. Finance receives controlled transaction records and audit trails. Multi-property leaders can compare performance while retaining local operating control.

Strategic valuePortfolio-wide visibility with consistent data and operating standards.
Operational valueFewer handoff failures across front office, housekeeping and service teams.
Commercial valueBetter rate, occupancy, channel and guest relationship decisions.
Designed forHotels, resorts, serviced apartments and multi-property hospitality groups.

Security & continuity

Protect guest, payment and operating data

HotelSync applies role-aware controls across front office, outlets, finance, management and group operations.

  • Role-based access and approval limits
  • Single sign-on with OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Audit trails for rates, folios, payments and configuration
  • Configurable privacy, retention and consent controls
  • Backups, high availability and disaster recovery
