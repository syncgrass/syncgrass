Executive summary

One health system view—from patient encounter to policy decision.

StateHIS provides a shared digital foundation for care delivery, facility operations and health-system intelligence. It supports clinical and administrative workflows at hospitals and PHCs while giving authorised leadership timely, comparable information across the state.

The platform is designed for state ministries of health, hospital management boards, primary healthcare agencies, facilities and public health programmes. A modular architecture supports phased adoption, interoperability and operations where connectivity varies.

Strategic valueStatewide visibility for policy, planning, funding and intervention management.
Operational valueConnected patient, appointment, pharmacy, inventory and facility workflows.
Clinical valueMore complete authorised context at the point of care.
Designed forHealth ministries, agencies, hospitals, PHCs and programme partners.

Privacy, security & safety

Protect sensitive health information by design

StateHIS applies layered safeguards to identity, clinical access, patient consent, data exchange, infrastructure and operational continuity.

  • Role, facility, programme and care-context access controls
  • SSO, strong authentication and session policies
  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Patient consent, purpose and configurable privacy controls
  • Detailed clinical, administrative and security audit trails
  • Backups, high availability, disaster recovery and downtime procedures
