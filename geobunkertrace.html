Executive summary

One governed picture of assets, threats, teams and action.

GeoBunkerTrace Enterprise gives operators, security organisations, regulators and executive leadership a shared operational environment for critical infrastructure. It connects pipeline networks, facilities, patrol activity, community and mobile reports, sensors, satellite observations, drone missions and CCTV evidence to location and time.

The platform replaces fragmented spreadsheets, phone calls and disconnected mapping tools with structured detection, verification, escalation, dispatch, investigation and closure workflows. Every decision is attributable. Every incident maintains a chain of evidence. Every role sees what it needs to act.

Strategic valuePortfolio-level risk visibility and evidence for investment, governance and stakeholder reporting.
Operational valueFaster validation, coordinated dispatch and a consistent operating picture across teams.
Business valueReduced avoidable loss, more focused field activity and stronger use of security resources.
Designed forOperators, regulators, command centres, security partners, field teams and executive leadership.

Enterprise security

Protection for the platform protecting your infrastructure

Security controls are applied across identity, application, data, infrastructure and operations, with configuration aligned to the deployment environment and organisational policies.

  • Role-based access control, segregation of duties and territory-aware permissions
  • Single sign-on through OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect
  • Encryption in transit and at rest with managed secret handling
  • Immutable audit trails, evidence integrity and security event monitoring
  • Backup, restore, high availability and disaster-recovery procedures
  • Configurable retention, classification and data-sovereignty controls
